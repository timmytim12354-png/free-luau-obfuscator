# Free Luarmor alternative for Roblox & Luau developers

KeyForge is a Roblox and Luau script authentication platform. Use it when you want keys, HWID locks, a dashboard, and optional ad-link monetization without starting from a paid whitelist host.

Live: [https://www.keyforge.win/luarmor-alternative](https://www.keyforge.win/luarmor-alternative) · Longer notes: [https://www.keyforge.win/compare/luarmor-alternatives](https://www.keyforge.win/compare/luarmor-alternatives)

## What KeyForge is

KeyForge (keyforge.win) is a Roblox and Luau script authentication, access-management, and protection platform. You store a script, choose how access works (keys, Discord, reward links, or keyless), copy a loader, and watch executions from the dashboard.

People looking for a Luarmor alternative usually want a place to lock a script behind a license, bind a device, and stop handing out raw source. KeyForge is built for that workflow. It is not the KeyForge card game.

## Why someone might choose KeyForge

- A free Starter plan so you can test a loader before paying
- Keys you can expire, revoke, and bind to HWID after first use
- Reward links for Linkvertise, LootLabs, and Work.ink
- Discord panels and slash commands
- Dashboard obfuscation (including wYnFuscate allowances by plan)

## KeyForge vs Luarmor (fair)

Luarmor is a well-known Roblox script protection and whitelist product. Confirm current Luarmor plans, limits, and features on **their official site** before you switch. This page does not invent Luarmor prices.

| Topic | KeyForge | Luarmor (verify on their site) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free plan | Starter is $0 with published project, script, and key caps | Typically sold as a paid protection service |
| Keys | Create, batch, expire, revoke | Whitelist / license-style access is core — confirm UX |
| HWID | Optional bind after first successful run | Device / HWID locking is commonly advertised |
| Ads | First-party Linkvertise / LootLabs / Work.ink gates | Confirm their monetization options |
| Obfuscation | Dashboard wYnFuscate / ForgeVM by plan | Confirm their protection pipeline |

**Is KeyForge a free Luarmor alternative?**  
KeyForge has a free Starter plan. Luarmor is generally sold as a paid protection service. Check both sites before you migrate.
